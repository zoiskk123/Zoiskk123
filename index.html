01 / About

A multidisciplinary perspective.

I am an emerging professional with an academic background in Business Administration, Marketing, Tourism and International Negotiations, and a strong interest in European affairs, cultural management and creative entrepreneurship.

My work brings together cultural heritage, educational initiatives, digital communication and creative publishing. I enjoy developing ideas into projects that connect people, encourage learning and create meaningful cultural experiences.

I am particularly interested in European institutions, international cooperation, innovation and the role of culture and education in society.
